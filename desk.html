Student Gate Entry / Exit

Main Gate · Security Desk
Ready. Scan a student barcode to record entry or exit automatically.
Automatic mode: first scan records EXIT (OUT). The next scan for that student records ENTRY (IN). The direction alternates with each valid scan. Your barcode scanner must send Enter after scanning. Duplicate scans within 10 seconds are ignored.
Reports are calculated from Gate_Logs and saved to separate sheets (Report_Daily and Report_Monthly). Student records and gate logs are never changed.
Saved only in this browser. Enter the Apps Script web app URL and the access token once.
